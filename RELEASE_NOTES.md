# Prevail 0.3.130

Every page now follows one layout: a sidebar you can collapse, a header
that stays in view, one full-width column, and small icon actions on each
row. Home has a new sidebar (Insights, Recommendations, Spark, Automations,
Calendar, Notes, then the Work board, Projects, Tasks and Goals). Source
Map is gone.

Recommendations is now the single "what to do next" page: one ranked list
across rules, projects, apps, people, models and context, grouped by
category down the side.

General is home. Start a conversation there and Prevail routes it to the
domains it belongs to, shown as chips you can remove; your corrections
teach the routing, and routed conversations also show in their domains.

Chat and council no longer open a context drawer on the right. The
Context button swaps the column for a Context view: sections down the
side, the one you pick fills the page, and Chat takes you back. The
Imports section only appears when a domain has imports.

Office green is the default accent everywhere, pages no longer grow wider
than the window, and an empty ideal-state answer no longer breaks Ideals.

No more side cards. Clicking a person, place, company or thing anywhere,
in chat or elsewhere, now opens the Entities view with it selected: the
list sits in a sidebar that collapses like Projects and Intent, and the
detail holds what the card had (what you've discussed, your notes, where
it was mentioned, what it comes up with, Save, Ask about it, Open map).
In Intent, the Capture button opens the capture setup in the page instead
of a drawer.

Entities, Intent and Apps keep their page header in view while you scroll,
and their content reads as one column (the rebuild check and the recipe's
tool list no longer split into side by side columns).

Every entity now has a page in your vault under data/entities, with its
conversations listed and room for your notes. Summaries are written for
the ones you save or that come up in three or more conversations.

Crash reports, when you turn them on, now carry only the error type and
where in the app it happened: never the message, which can quote a prompt,
a chat, a name or a vault path. Usage analytics pass only the fixed list
of anonymous events.

Engine 1.9.30.
