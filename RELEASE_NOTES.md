# Prevail 0.4.0

A focus release. Prevail does fewer things, each one is easier to find, and
every page now works the same way: tabs across the top, a column of items,
and the one you pick in full on the right.

Home is quiet: the headline and the composer. Search lives in the sidebar
(Cmd-K), and what is waiting for you shows as a count on the Inbox row.
Spark, Automations, Calendar, Notes and the Work board are gone from the
app. Nothing was deleted: your loops keep running on your hub Mac and your
notes stay in the vault.

Chat with anything. Every person, place, company and thing now has its own
conversation that builds over time, with what you know about it in front of
the model on every turn. Save a reply to its notes in one click, and find
past conversations on its page.

Goals holds your mission and vision alongside the goals you add over time,
each with why it matters, a target date and progress. Click any part of your
mission to rewrite it; every save keeps the earlier text, marked by date,
with the newest marked Latest, and any version can be restored.

Tasks, Goals and the Inbox share the same layout, and a task opens in the
page instead of a side panel. The Inbox sorts what needs your answer into
Actions, Google, Automations and Tasks. Allow, Always and Deny work as
before, and every approval still takes a single-use token.

Settings is down to 11 entries. Connections, Privacy & Safety and Settings
are one page each, Tools, Skills and Frameworks are one Toolkit, Usage sits
inside Activity, and Council and Arena match Models.

Your apps are in the sidebar, each with a small mark for the runtime it
connects through. The Context view shows a section as soon as you pick it,
previews files in place, and shows the folder it reads from. Text sizes are
consistent across every page.

Engine 1.10.0.
