# Prevail 0.3.131

Answer held actions right in the conversation. When the agent wants to do
something that needs your OK, a small card appears under its reply with
Allow, Always and Deny. Allow runs it once and the agent carries on. Deny
tells the agent no, and it will not try the same thing again. The card and
the Inbox stay in step, so answering in one clears the other.

Always saves a rule for that tool in that domain, so the agent stops
asking. It is only offered for low-risk actions like edits, drafts and
saves. Anything that sends to someone, spends money, deletes or touches a
password always asks. Your rules are listed under Privacy, Always allowed,
where you can remove any of them.

See what is waiting for you at a glance: conversations, domains and Work
board tasks that need your answer show it, and Home shows the count and
takes you to the Inbox.

Schedule a conversation. The calendar icon in the chat header runs a
prompt daily, on weekdays, weekly or at a time you pick, and each reply is
added to that conversation. Your schedules are listed in Automations,
where you can run one now, pause it or remove it. They run on your hub
Mac while it keeps working with the app closed.
