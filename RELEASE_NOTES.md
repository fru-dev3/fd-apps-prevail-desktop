# Prevail 0.3.126

A small follow-up to the new Apps screen.

## Real logos

Connectors whose icon lives on another host, like Google Calendar and
Privacy.com, now show their own logo instead of a letter. Icon fetches only
ever reach public sites over https, never your local network.

## Only your connectors

Codex's own helper tools no longer appear in the Apps list next to the
connectors you signed into. Requires engine 1.9.26, bundled with this release.
